### Artificial intelligence and automation


After the Presentation on Artificial intelligence and automation now I'm seeing AI as a more critical and realistic issue. I mostly saw AI as an exciting innovation that is making life easier.  In the present, I discuss the historical webs of AI and they help me understand that the fear of technology replacing humans is not new. Almost in every shift of technology the nature of work has changed but with time society has also adapted it.  And then I realized that AI is not something that will only take over human work but It's also about how humans choose to use it.


One of the aspects of this topic that I found really interesting was the debate of unemployment and jobs that will vanish by AI. They talked about AI is not only about taking jobs but also about giving jobs like Prompt engineers and many more related to AI.


The part of the presentation that I felt was more interesting was the historical context with present day examples as it makes the topic easier to relate to in today's world.


As an IS student I feel more connected to this topic because I want to do a job tech in future.  It made me realize that my future career and technology may not be the same as now. I realised that for a career in technology developing AI systems and understanding their ethical and social impact is more necessary than just by learning traditional courses.
