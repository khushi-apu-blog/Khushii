### Learning Through Chatbot and Data Visualization Projects



In both chatbot and Metabase initially I felt difficulty because it was new to me and also it is way too different from the traditional theory based learning.That’s why I was so worried initially as both tools were completely new to me. First I went through all the instructions, then I started working and exploring, trying and getting to know things on my own which somehow troubled me but it is also more engaging and also challenging.


Another thing that I noticed is how planning for each project works . I have made a chatbot based on Umnukt since I am also a fresher so I can relate to it better and that's why it has become easier for me to design a conversation that is relatable for freshers.  But still organizing the flow of the conversation is harder than I expected. Initially I was very confused about how to organize different elements like the text, how to insert pictures and many more. But I improved myself by trying again and again.


In the Metabase dashboard project I learned how raw data can be turned into something that can be understood by the public easily. Initially when I saw the data it was so overwhelming but when I started framing questions for visualizing the data It was not that much complicated.  I started creating charts and graphs on the basis of what visualization I wanted from questions.  And that approach of starting with the questions and then making the charts based on that was very helpful for me.


The main takeaway from the projects is that we learn something more when we actually do that rather than just reading or writing. I realized that mistakes shape our learning.
